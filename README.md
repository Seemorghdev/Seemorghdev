## Work you can open

- **Germany job-ease index.** Which German cities have the most job openings for each person looking for work? Using official Federal Employment Agency statistics for October 2026, Ulm leads 68 cities with 5.3× Berlin’s rate. Python, DuckDB, dbt and Dagster.
  [Case study](https://mehridian.tech/projects/germany-job-ease-index) · [Map](https://mehridian.tech/projects/job-ease-map/index.htm) · [Source](https://github.com/Seemorghdev/germany-job-ease-index)
- **Small Objects in Big Frames.** Can a small detector find a drone a few dozen pixels wide? Fine-tuning it for small drones (v7) raised its first pick on public drone video from 12.6% to 59.4%.
  [Case study](https://mehridian.tech/projects/small-object-detection) · [Model](https://huggingface.co/Seemorgh/yolo11n-aod4-v7-small-targets)
- **Seemorgh operator console.** Five sensors that disagree on position, time and identity, combined into one air picture. A recorded 91-step synthetic scenario you can scrub through.
  [Case study](https://mehridian.tech/projects/seemorgh-operator-console) · [Replay](https://console.apps.mehridian.tech/)
- **Edge Evidence.** Storing evidence so that a copy only counts once it has been read back and matched to the original. A read-only inspector over a synthetic demo.
  [Case study](https://mehridian.tech/projects/edge-evidence) · [Inspector](https://edge-evidence.apps.mehridian.tech/) · [Source](https://github.com/Seemorghdev/edge-evidence-reference-platform-public)
- **What 15 repositories prove.** I audited my own repositories. Green checks were nearly everywhere; proof that the work had been accepted, or ever ran, almost nowhere.
  [Study](https://mehridian.tech/research/project03/)

I use AI coding agents for much of the implementation, testing and documentation. I set the design and the acceptance criteria, and I review each change before it merges. That makes the activity graph busy, so judge the work by the code, the tests and the write-ups, not the commit count. [More on the workflow](ENGINEERING-WORKFLOW.md).
